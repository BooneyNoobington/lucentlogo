# Kronos design prompt and production notes

The concept was created with the built-in image generation tool. The final deliverables were rebuilt as clean SVG geometry and rendered to PNG, following the same workflow used for lucentLIMS.

The approved direction was A: a calendar outline with two binding tabs, a header divider and a diagonal telephone handset in place of the date grid. The user supplied the name Kronos and requested the Baden-Württemberg public-service colour scheme.

## Final colour concept prompt

+Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand.
Input image 1 is the edit target: the blue-and-teal Kronos concept board. Preserve concept A's calendar-and-telephone-handset design and the exact wordmark "Kronos".
The user has corrected the required color scheme to Baden-Württemberg PUBLIC SERVICES corporate design (related to THE LÄND).
Change the palette and app-icon background treatment as follows:
- BaWü Gelb is EXACTLY #FFFC00, a bright near-lemon yellow.
- BaWü Schwarz is EXACTLY #2A2623, a warm almost-black.
- White is #FFFFFF.
Replace ALL blue and teal in the mark with solid warmblack #2A2623. Place the entire black calendar outline, two black short rounded binding tabs, black header divider, and large solid black diagonal telephone handset on ONE flat yellow rounded-square app tile. Yellow behind the symbol gives reliable contrast to every detail. Use generous space between the calendar and the app tile edges. Modest tile corner radius. Calendar retains transparent/negative yellow interior, not white. Preserve the simple recognizable calendar-and-handset structure. No added clock, date cells, arrows, state crest, lions, government logo or campaign wording.
Set the exact wordmark "Kronos" at right in warmblack clean contemporary semibold sans-serif.
Presentation: spacious white landscape sheet, a large horizontal logo (yellow tile with black calendar/handset at left and Kronos at right). Below, a small row shows the SAME yellow-tile icon and a warmblack transparent-style standalone calendar/handset mark without a tile. No other text. All repeated symbols identical.
Strict flat solid fills, perfectly crisp vector-like shapes. Remove gradients, paper, grain, shading, highlights, shadows and texture. No 3D, no physical mockups. The only colors are #FFFC00, #2A2623 and white. No blue or teal anywhere.
Text verbatim: "Kronos".

## Final vector artwork

The final vectors use BaWü Gelb #FFFC00, BaWü Schwarz #2A2623 and white #FFFFFF. The warmblack mark is placed on a yellow rounded-square app tile. Separate warmblack and white standalone marks have no tile. The wordmark is outlined from Avenir Next Demi Bold. The final master SVG uses flat fills, a consistent calendar stroke and a solid handset silhouette. No fonts or generated raster textures are embedded in the SVGs.

Official palette source: https://design.landbw.de/corporate-design/basiselemente/farbe/farbwerte (checked 30 October 2026).
