# Kronos logo kit

Kronos is an on-call scheduling app. The logo combines the approved calendar-and-handset concept with Baden-Württemberg's public-service colour palette.

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | RGB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BaWü Gelb | `#FFFC00` | 255, 252, 0 |
| BaWü Schwarz | `#2A2623` | 42, 38, 35 |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 |

Source: [Baden-Württemberg Design Portal — Farbwerte](https://design.landbw.de/corporate-design/basiselemente/farbe/farbwerte), checked 30 October 2026. The public-service palette uses a warmer black than the THE LÄND campaign; the yellow is shared.

## Files and use

- `kronos-logo.svg` / `.png`: primary horizontal logo, yellow app tile and warmblack wordmark, transparent surrounding area.
- `kronos-logo-reversed.svg` / `.png`: primary logo with a white wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `kronos-logo-monochrome.svg` / `.png`: warmblack symbol and wordmark with no tile.
- `kronos-logo-monochrome-reversed.svg` / `.png`: white symbol and wordmark with no tile.
- `kronos-icon.svg`: scalable yellow app tile with warmblack calendar and handset.
- `kronos-icon-{size}.png`: app icon at 16, 32, 48, 64, 180, 192, 512 and 1024 pixels.
- `kronos-icon-monochrome.svg` / `kronos-icon-monochrome-512.png`: warmblack symbol on transparent background.
- `kronos-icon-reversed.svg` / `kronos-icon-reversed-512.png`: white symbol on transparent background.
- `favicon.svg` / `favicon.ico`: browser favicon; ICO contains 16, 32 and 48 pixel versions.
- `kronos-preview.png`: final artwork on light and dark backgrounds, including actual-size favicon samples.
- `kronos-concept.png`: the image-generated colour exploration that informed the final vectors.
- `kronos-design-prompts.md`: final image-generation prompt and production notes.

The SVG wordmark is outlined from Avenir Next Demi Bold, so no font installation is needed. All final PNGs are rendered from the vector artwork. The calendar and handset use the same geometry across the tile and monochrome versions.

Use the yellow-tile version for app icons and favicons. Use warmblack standalone artwork on white or yellow, and the white standalone version on dark backgrounds. The yellow tile itself is opaque; the area outside its rounded corners is transparent.
